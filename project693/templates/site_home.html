Welcome to the Ornamental Preference Survey

The Ornamental Preference Survey is a short interactive survey designed to understand how everyday people choose between different ornamental plants, especially what floral attributes make certain plants more attractive.

You'll be shown ten pairs of plants and will be asked to click on the one you prefer. There is no right or wrong answer, what is wanted is your initial impression of which one you prefer. There is then a final question to help us to understand your reasoning.

Your participation in this survey will help researchers at Lincoln University, New Zealand explore public preferences for ornamental plants.
